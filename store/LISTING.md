# Peelbox — Galaxy Store listing

## App title
Peelbox: Sticker Keyboard

## Short description (≤ 80 characters)
Keep your own stickers in packs and send them as real stickers anywhere.

## Description
Made a sticker with Galaxy AI, cut one out of a photo, or saved a GIF you love? Peelbox gives your stickers a home — and sends them as real stickers, not flat photos with a black box around them.

**Collect**
• Paste stickers straight from the Samsung Keyboard's sticker tab into a pack
• Share images and GIFs to Peelbox from any app, or add files in bulk
• Animated GIF, animated WebP and APNG keep their animation; transparency stays intact

**Organize**
• Sort stickers into packs, drag to reorder, pick a cover
• Move stickers between packs; duplicates are caught automatically

**Send**
• The Peelbox keyboard sends your stickers the way a keyboard does, so apps like Messenger show them as real stickers
• Switch to it in one tap with the Quick Settings tile

**Back up and share**
• Export packs as .stickerpack files or plain image folders, and import them on another phone
• Works with Palette for Obsidian, which can install Peelbox packs

**Private by design**
Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking. The Peelbox keyboard has no keys and never sees what you type.

**Fair pricing**
Free for up to 3 packs. Peelbox Unlimited is a one-time purchase — no subscription, ever.
