Peelbox Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Peelbox is a sticker organizer and sticker keyboard. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

What Peelbox stores

The stickers you add and the packs you make are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or to any server. If you export a pack, the file goes only where you choose to save or share it.

The Peelbox keyboard

The Peelbox keyboard has no keys and cannot read, record or transmit anything you type. Its only function is to insert the sticker you tap into the app you're using. Android shows a standard warning for every keyboard saying it "may be able to collect all the text you type"; Peelbox does not.

What we collect

Nothing. Peelbox has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Peelbox Unlimited" purchase is handled entirely by Samsung In-App Purchase through the Galaxy Store. Samsung processes the payment and may collect information such as your Samsung account and device identifiers under Samsung's privacy policy. Peelbox only receives whether the purchase was completed, and remembers that on your device.

Permissions

Peelbox uses the internet only to talk to Samsung In-App Purchase. It does not request access to your photos, contacts, location, microphone or camera; images reach the app only when you paste, share or pick them yourself.

Children

Peelbox does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
